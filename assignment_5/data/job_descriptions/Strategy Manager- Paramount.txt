The Manager, Strategy will support the development and execution of the growth strategy for Paramount Products & Experiences. The role requires strategic and analytical thinking, proficient modeling capabilities, and remarkable collaboration and communication skills. The successful candidate will possess the ability to interface effectively with multiple levels of management across the business and Paramount.

Responsibilities

Collaborate and coordinate with members of the strategy and business teams in the development and execution of key global growth initiatives - particularly as they relate to new media platforms, new business opportunities, and geographic expansion
Proactively identify new growth opportunities (e.g., new product categories/channels, alternate business models, emerging trends)
Construct financial/analytical models in support of various strategic opportunities, potential acquisitions, and other financial deals. Summarize and communicate key learnings to senior management
Identify, define, and analyze trends/strategies across major markets in relation to the consumer products (e.g., toys, apparel, home, CPG), publishing, and experiences (e.g., theme parks) industries
In partnership with Retail Analytics, identify areas of research and perform appropriate qualitative and quantitative analyses; perform primary and secondary research as needed
Support the creation of compelling, persuasive presentation materials to communicate key learnings, outcomes, strategies, and recommendations to senior level executives and key external partners
Participate in variety of meetings with professionalism and credibility

Basic Qualifications

2+ years of experience in strategic planning, consulting, banking, or relevant industry/function
Exemplary quantitative and analytical skills, including financial modeling

Additional Qualifications

Superior strategic and business acumen
Outstanding verbal and written communications skills, particularly the ability to develop arguments and present them concisely to a range of stakeholders, including executives
Strong work ethic and attention to detail
Outstanding multi-tasking and teamwork capabilities
